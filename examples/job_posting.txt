Data Engineer (Mid-level) — Brightline Analytics

About the role
Brightline Analytics helps retailers understand their sales data. We are looking
for a Data Engineer to build and maintain the pipelines that feed our dashboards.

What you will do
- Design, build and maintain batch data pipelines.
- Write and optimize SQL queries over large datasets.
- Work with analysts to turn business questions into reliable data models.
- Monitor pipeline health and fix data quality issues.
- Document pipelines so the rest of the team can use them.

Must have
- 3+ years of experience as a data engineer or in a similar role
- Strong Python
- Strong SQL
- Experience with Apache Airflow or another workflow scheduler
- Experience with a cloud data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift)
- Docker
- Good written communication

Nice to have
- dbt
- Kubernetes
- Experience in retail or e-commerce

We offer a hybrid schedule, learning budget and a friendly team.
